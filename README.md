# 🚖 Executive Overview of Bangladeshi Ride-Sharing Platforms

![Dashboard Preview](Interactive%20Dashboard%20Bangladesh.jpg)

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides an executive overview of the Bangladeshi ride-sharing market, evaluating operational performance and revenue metrics across major platforms including Uber, Pathao, inDrive, and OBHAI. Powered by custom DAX measures and dynamic slicers for platform and city, the report tracks core KPIs such as total completed bookings (8K), ride revenue (2M), average fare (210.69), and cancellation rates (0.13), while breaking down regional revenue dominance in Dhaka and Chattogram, preferred payment methods, and fleet distribution across bikes, cars, and CNGs.

## 📊 What is inside the dashboard
- 📈 **Core Metrics:** Tracks 8K completed bookings, 2M in completed ride revenue, an average fare of 210.69, 1K cancellations (0.13 cancellation rate), 61.61K KM in estimated distance, and 327.67K in platform commissions[cite: 1, 3].
- 🏆 **Platform Comparison:** Breaks down booking share across Pathao (37.28%), Uber (34.36%), inDrive (20.71%), and OBHAI (7.65%), alongside each platform's share of total cancellations[cite: 1, 3].
- 🏙️ **City and Payment Splits:** Shows revenue distribution by city—with Dhaka leading at 56.42% and Chattogram at 18.47%—and compares revenue across Cash, bKash, Card, and Nagad[cite: 1, 3].
- 🏍️ **Vehicle Types:** Compares completed bookings across Bikes, Cars, and CNGs for each operator[cite: 1, 3].
- 🎛️ **Interactive Slicers:** Includes dropdown filters at the top right so users can filter the entire report by a specific Platform or City[cite: 1, 3].

## 📑 Data Source & Methodology
This project uses a synthetic dataset of 10,000 ride-booking records generated for educational and business intelligence practice. The dataset structure and analytical assumptions were informed by publicly available research on Bangladesh's ride-sharing industry. Platform names and service categories provide industry context; however, all transaction records, financial figures, operational metrics, platform distributions and performance indicators are hypothetical and do not represent actual company data or market shares.

## Project Status
Completed as a practical Business Intelligence and data analytics portfolio project.

## 💡 Analytical Objectives/Highlights
- Evaluate booking and ride completion performance.
- Compare platform-level operational indicators.
- Identify major ride cancellation patterns.
- Examine geographical and time-based demand.
- Analyse revenue distribution and financial performance.
- Present actionable business insights through interactive reporting.
